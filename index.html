Our story · เรื่องของเรา

Five years of “Tu Kafae”.

We opened our first little coffee shop on 24 September 2021. Five years later, ตู้กาแฟ 2Kaffee has three branches around Khon Kaen, and we're still the same small shop at heart: a friendly bar, coffee we care about, and walls full of things we love to look at.

Step inside and you'll find tall shelves of jars, black-and-white photographs, a Marilyn mural, chandeliers and a collection of classic model cars parked between the cups. Come for a quick takeaway on your way to work, or stay a while.

House coffee · กาแฟของเรา

Four jars. Four characters.

Our four main coffees are blended by our own owner. You can try all of them at every branch.

Dark roast · คั่วเข้ม

Black Matter

Our only dark roast. Dark but not bitter, smooth but full-bodied. A blend of three beans: Nan robusta, Brazil arabica and Doi Chang.

Medium roast · คั่วกลาง

Fruity Pop

Medium roast with a brighter, fruit-forward cup.

Medium roast · คั่วกลาง

Rio Blend

Our everyday medium roast, balanced and easy-drinking.

Light roast · คั่วอ่อน

Ethi Blend

Light roast for those who like it delicate and aromatic.

Bags of our beans are coming soon. Ask at the bar or follow us on Facebook.
